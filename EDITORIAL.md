# The Usurper League — Editorial voice

## The premise

The money clubs can sod off. We're having a league.

The Usurper League is a joyful, slightly impudent alternative football table. We take the matches involving a league's dominant money clubs out of the record, recalculate the remaining clubs' results and see what happens. For Spain, that means **Real Madrid and FC Barcelona**, with their fixtures, goals and points removed. The full-table comparison remains a *calculation*, not LaLiga's official ranking.

**We're not running a protest.** We're hosting the better party in the field next door. Visitors should feel a sense of possibility rather than resentment.

## Voice — dry, not desperate

- British-style deadpan, with a little Alexander-style mischief. A raised eyebrow beats a punchline.
- Short sentences; normal words; confident rhythm. A joke should arrive incidentally.
- The numbers are serious. The competition is invented. Never blur the distinction.
- One quietly funny observation per block is plenty. Statistics labels stay legible.
- Never sound like an AI trying to write sports banter: avoid hype, emojis, "epic", "ultimate", "game-changing", "revolutionary", "literally", and breathless declarations.
- Avoid punching down or sneering at supporters of excluded clubs. The money and the inevitability of rich-club dominance are the targets. Everyone is still welcome to read.
- Keep jokes evergreen. Don't make claims that would become false on the next update.

## Examples

Good:
- **THE USURPER LEAGUE**
- *The money clubs can sod off. We're having a league.*
- "Apparently, other clubs can win things."
- "Yes, yes. Real Madrid and Barcelona are very good at football. We've all noticed."
- "They can go and play in their own sandpit."
- "Same football. Much more interesting."
- "The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not."
- "The gods have been excused."

Too much:
- "BREAKING: The football revolution begins! The giants have fallen!"
- "We're taking the beautiful game BACK from evil billionaires!!!"
- "The most incredible alternative league on the internet."

## English and Spanish

Write Spanish **as Spanish**, not as a line-by-line translation. Preserve the joke and the level of irreverence.

English:
- "The money clubs can sod off. We're having a league."
- "The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not."

Spanish:
- "Que les den a los clubes millonarios. Nosotros montamos nuestra liga."
- "Los resultados son reales. La copa, por desgracia, no."

Keep the global brand **The Usurper League** in both languages. *La Liga de los Mortales* is the name of the Spanish competition within it, not the website's parent brand.

## Factual safeguards

- All excluded matches are deleted in full: points, goals, wins, losses and games played.
- No scores, match results, historical winners or club names may be invented. When uncertain, say so.
- Historical seasons through 1994/95 use **two** points for a win; from 1995/96, **three**.
- Historically unusual formats and incomplete archives must be identified explicitly.
- Our positions use points, goal difference and goals scored and may differ from LaLiga's head-to-head tiebreak order.
- Sponsorship copy, merchandise and external media need separate review for rights, advertising and accuracy.

## Publishing

There are currently no standalone long-form articles in the repository. The front page contains automated live and historical editorial summaries. Apply this guide to any future articles, social posts, SEO snippets and league expansions. Keep the underlying data logic independent of prose.

The GitHub repository is still named `the-real-league` and the temporary deployment uses `the-real-league.pages.dev`. Do not rename the repository or move the canonical URL until the new domain is purchased, connected and verified.
