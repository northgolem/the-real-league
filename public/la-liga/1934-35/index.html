02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Real Betis ranked 1st in the all-club calculation, with 34 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 28 points from 18 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
