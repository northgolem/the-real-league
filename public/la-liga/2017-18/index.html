01 / THE TITLE RACE

Atlético Madrid vs Valencia

Atlético Madrid finish 5 points ahead of Valencia (71 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Alavés make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 12th to 10th, up 2 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

74 real fixtures gone

Atlético Madrid ranked 2nd in the published complete table with 79 points. Remove Madrid and Barça fixtures and they finish with 76 from 34 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
