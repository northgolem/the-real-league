THINGS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGED / 2009/10

Three notes from this parallel universe.

These are calculated from this season's results, not a fictional match report.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Valencia vs RCD Mallorca

Valencia finish 8 points ahead of RCD Mallorca (62 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Real Zaragoza make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 12th to 10th, up 2 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

74 real fixtures gone

Valencia ranked 3rd in the all-club calculation, with 71 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 70 points from 34 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
