La Liga 2021/22.
Without the usual suspects.

Sevilla FC win our alternative 2021/22 La Liga on 69 points from 34 matches. Atlético Madrid follow on 65 points. That's a 4-point lead over second place. The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not.

Yes, the two biggest clubs are very good at football. We know. For this table, we've removed every match involving Real Madrid or FC Barcelona. The remaining fixtures keep their actual scores. In our full-league calculation they were 4th, rather than first.

A note about the numbers
