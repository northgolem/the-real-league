THINGS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGED / 2002/03

Three notes from this parallel universe.

These are calculated from this season's results, not a fictional match report.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Real Sociedad vs Deportivo La Coruña

Real Sociedad finish 4 points ahead of Deportivo La Coruña (65 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Espanyol make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 15th to 10th, up 5 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

74 real fixtures gone

Real Sociedad ranked 2nd in the all-club calculation, with 76 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 69 points from 34 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
