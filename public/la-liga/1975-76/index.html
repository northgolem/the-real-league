THINGS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGED / 1975/76

Three notes from this parallel universe.

These are calculated from this season's results, not a fictional match report.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Atlético Madrid vs Espanyol

Atlético Madrid and Espanyol both finish on 38 points. We separate them by goal difference and then goals scored.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Sevilla FC make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 9th to 8th, up 1 place. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

66 real fixtures gone

Atlético Madrid ranked 3rd in the all-club calculation, with 42 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 38 points from 30 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
