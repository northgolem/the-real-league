La Liga 2011/12.
Take Madrid and Barça out.

Here's the idea: We remove Real Madrid, Barcelona and every match either played. Then we recalculate the league using only the actual results between the clubs left. No invented wins, no donated points.

Valencia finish top of our alternative 2011/12 La Liga with 59 points. Málaga are next on 57. Not an official championship. A different way of counting the same football.
