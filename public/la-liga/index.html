THE RULES

No invented results. No rich-kid fixtures.

We remove all matches played by Madrid or Barça. That means points, goals, wins, losses, the lot. We recalculate the surviving teams using each era's points system.

Seasons with incomplete archival results aren't published. La Liga wasn't played during the 1936–39 Civil War, and our archive lacks a complete match source for 1962/63.

Tables use points, goal difference and goals scored, rather than every historic official tiebreaker or deduction. This is a thought experiment, not a correction of the record.
