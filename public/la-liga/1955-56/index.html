01 / THE TITLE RACE

Athletic Club vs Sevilla FC

Athletic Club finish 11 points ahead of Sevilla FC (31 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Espanyol make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 5th to 4th, up 1 place. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

58 real fixtures gone

Athletic Club ranked 1st in the all-club calculation, with 48 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 42 points from 26 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
