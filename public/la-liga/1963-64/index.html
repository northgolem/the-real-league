THINGS THAT ACTUALLY CHANGED / 1963/64

Three notes from this parallel universe.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Real Betis vs Valencia

Real Betis finish 2 points ahead of Valencia (32 points). A proper scrap, then.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Valencia make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 4th to 2nd, up 2 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

58 real fixtures gone

Real Betis ranked 3rd in the all-club calculation, with 37 points. After removing fixtures involving Madrid or Barça, they have 34 points from 26 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
