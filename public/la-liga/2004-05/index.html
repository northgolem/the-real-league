Take Madrid and Barça out.

Here's the idea: We remove Real Madrid, Barcelona and every match either played. Then we recalculate the league using only the actual results between the clubs left. No invented wins, no donated points.

Villarreal finish 3 points ahead of Real Betis (57 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Real Sociedad make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 12th to 8th, up 4 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

74 real fixtures gone
