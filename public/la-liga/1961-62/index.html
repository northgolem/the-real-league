La Liga 1961/62.
Without the usual suspects.

Atlético Madrid win our alternative 1961/62 La Liga on 33 points from 26 matches. Athletic Club follow on 30 points. That's a 3-point lead over second place. The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not.

Yes, the two biggest clubs are very good at football. We know. For this table, we've removed every match involving Real Madrid or FC Barcelona. The remaining fixtures keep their actual scores. In our full-league calculation they were 3rd, rather than first.

A note about the numbers

These are independent recalculations, not official LaLiga standings. Positions are ordered by points, goal difference and goals scored. Historic points deductions and the league's official head-to-head tiebreakers aren't recreated, so some rankings may differ from the official record.
