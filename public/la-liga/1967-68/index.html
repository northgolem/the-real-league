01 / THE TITLE RACE

UD Las Palmas vs Athletic Club

UD Las Palmas finish 5 points ahead of Athletic Club (30 points). Comfortable enough in this version of events.

02 / WHO MOVED?

Movement among the survivors

Athletic Club make the biggest climb among the remaining clubs: from 5th to 2nd, up 3 places. Both positions compare only the clubs still in our league.

03 / THE PRICE OF EXCLUSION

58 real fixtures gone

UD Las Palmas ranked 3rd in the published complete table with 38 points. Remove Madrid and Barça fixtures and they finish with 35 from 26 matches. They weren't handed anyone else's points. Neither was anyone else.
