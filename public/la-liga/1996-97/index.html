La Liga 1996/97.
Without the usual suspects.

Deportivo La Coruña win our alternative 1996/97 La Liga on 76 points from 38 matches. Real Betis follow on 75 points. That's a 1-point lead over second place. The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not.

Yes, the two biggest clubs are very good at football. We know. For this table, we've removed every match involving Real Madrid or FC Barcelona. The remaining fixtures keep their actual scores. In our full-league calculation they were 4th, rather than first.

A note about the numbers

These are independent recalculations, not official LaLiga standings. Positions are ordered by points, goal difference and goals scored. Historic points deductions and the league's official head-to-head tiebreakers aren't recreated, so some rankings may differ from the official record.
