La Liga 1999/00.
Without the usual suspects.

Deportivo La Coruña win our alternative 1999/00 La Liga on 62 points from 34 matches. Real Zaragoza follow on 59 points. That's a 3-point lead over second place. The scores are real. The trophy, regrettably, is not.

Yes, the two biggest clubs are very good at football. We know. For this table, we've removed every match involving Real Madrid or FC Barcelona. The remaining fixtures keep their actual scores. In our full-league calculation they were 1st, rather than first.

A note about the numbers
