How we stole the league

01 / THE EXITSTwo clubs. One polite goodbye.

Real Madrid and Barcelona have their own table. This one is for everyone else.

02 / THE ERASERTheir results go with them.

Every fixture against either club disappears. Points, goals, games played. We don’t do half-measures.

03 / THE FUNThen things get interesting.

Everyone else keeps their actual results. No invented scores, no fantasy transfers. Just a different champion.
