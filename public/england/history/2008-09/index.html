THREE THINGS FROM 2008/09

What actually changed?

These observations come from the standings for this season. No alternate-universe scorelines required.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Everton vs Aston Villa

Everton finish 1 points ahead of Aston Villa, who have 50. Not much room for complacency.

02 / WHO MOVED?

The biggest climb

Blackburn Rovers make the biggest climb among the clubs still in the division: from 9 to 4, up 5 places. That's a comparison against the same surviving teams, not a free jump just because we deleted six names.

03 / THE MISSING MATCHES

198 fixtures deleted

We kept 182 of the 380 real results. Everton had 63 points with everybody included; 12 came from matches we've now removed. Nobody inherited them.

About the numbers

These are not official Premier League standings. Wins earn three points; ties are ranked by goal difference and goals scored. Historic official deductions and further tiebreakers aren't necessarily reproduced.

Original completed results: footballcsv/england, CC0-licensed data. The exclusion rule applies only to clubs actually in the top division for that season.
