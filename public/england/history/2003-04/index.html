THREE THINGS FROM 2003/04

What actually changed?

These observations come from the standings for this season. No alternate-universe scorelines required.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Aston Villa vs Newcastle United

Aston Villa finish 5 points ahead of Newcastle United, who have 43. A healthier lead than the arguments in the comments, probably.

02 / WHO MOVED?

The biggest climb

Blackburn Rovers make the biggest climb among the clubs still in the division: from 10 to 7, up 3 places. That's a comparison against the same surviving teams, not a free jump just because we deleted six names.

03 / THE MISSING MATCHES

198 fixtures deleted

We kept 182 of the 380 real results. Aston Villa had 56 points with everybody included; 8 came from matches we've now removed. Nobody inherited them.

About the numbers

These are not official Premier League standings. Wins earn three points; ties are ranked by goal difference and goals scored. Historic official deductions and further tiebreakers aren't necessarily reproduced.

Original completed results: footballcsv/england, CC0-licensed data. The exclusion rule applies only to clubs actually in the top division for that season.

If you'd have kicked someone else out, good news: that's now an option.
