THREE THINGS FROM 1998/99

What actually changed?

These observations come from the standings for this season. No alternate-universe scorelines required.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Leeds United vs Aston Villa

Leeds United finish 9 points ahead of Aston Villa, who have 45. A healthier lead than the arguments in the comments, probably.

02 / WHO MOVED?

The biggest climb

Newcastle United make the biggest climb among the clubs still in the division: from 8 to 3, up 5 places. That's a comparison against the same surviving teams, not a free jump just because we deleted six names.

03 / THE MISSING MATCHES

170 fixtures deleted

We kept 210 of the 380 real results. Leeds United had 67 points with everybody included; 13 came from matches we've now removed. Nobody inherited them.

Not in the division: Manchester City wasn't playing in the Premier League that year. So there were no fixtures involving those clubs to remove.

About the numbers

These are not official Premier League standings. Wins earn three points; ties are ranked by goal difference and goals scored. Historic official deductions and further tiebreakers aren't necessarily reproduced.

Original completed results: footballcsv/england, CC0-licensed data. The exclusion rule applies only to clubs actually in the top division for that season.

If you'd have kicked someone else out, good news: that's now an option.
