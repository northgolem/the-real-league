THREE THINGS FROM 2017/18

What actually changed?

These observations come from the standings for this season. No alternate-universe scorelines required.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Burnley vs Everton

Burnley finish 2 points ahead of Everton, who have 45. Not much room for complacency.

02 / WHO MOVED?

The biggest climb

Crystal Palace make the biggest climb among the clubs still in the division: from 5 to 3, up 2 places. That's a comparison against the same surviving teams, not a free jump just because we deleted six names.

03 / THE MISSING MATCHES

198 fixtures deleted

We kept 182 of the 380 real results. Burnley had 54 points with everybody included; 7 came from matches we've now removed. Nobody inherited them.

About the numbers

These are not official Premier League standings. Wins earn three points; ties are ranked by goal difference and goals scored. Historic official deductions and further tiebreakers aren't necessarily reproduced.

Original completed results: openfootball/football.json, CC0-licensed data. The exclusion rule applies only to clubs actually in the top division for that season.
