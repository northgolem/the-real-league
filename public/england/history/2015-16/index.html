THREE THINGS FROM 2015/16

What actually changed?

These observations come from the standings for this season. No alternate-universe scorelines required.

01 / THE TITLE RACE

Leicester City vs Southampton

Leicester City finish 21 points ahead of Southampton, who have 43. A healthier lead than the arguments in the comments, probably.

02 / WHO MOVED?

The biggest climb

Watford make the biggest climb among the clubs still in the division: from 7 to 3, up 4 places. That's a comparison against the same surviving teams, not a free jump just because we deleted six names.

03 / THE MISSING MATCHES

198 fixtures deleted

We kept 182 of the 380 real results. Leicester City had 81 points with everybody included; 17 came from matches we've now removed. Nobody inherited them.

About the numbers

These are not official Premier League standings. Wins earn three points; ties are ranked by goal difference and goals scored. Historic official deductions and further tiebreakers aren't necessarily reproduced.

Original completed results: openfootball/football.json, CC0-licensed data. The exclusion rule applies only to clubs actually in the top division for that season.
