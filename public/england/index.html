You choose the guest list.

All their matches go too.

No cherry-picking. Every fixture involving a club you exclude disappears completely, including its goals, points and games played.

The rest keep playing.

Everyone else's real results stay intact. Three points for a win, one for a draw. Nothing made up. We're cheeky, not cheats.
